Simulated sensors, terrain and faults, built to illustrate the method in the GNSSSentinel proposal. These are not field logs. On the two hills the slope blocks part of the sky and hillside reflections bias the fix; the receiver does not report that bias, so the trust monitor has to catch it. Noise, bias and fault magnitudes are modelled on a low-cost receiver, wheel encoders and a MEMS IMU.
